In a world of intertwined steel and magic where humans, elves, and beastkin coexist, the young swordswoman Althea embarks on a journey burdened with the destiny of stopping the Demon Lord from destroying the world. Along the way she 
meets a proud elven archer from the deep forests and a gentle shaman from the beastkin tribes, and through battles, secrets, and shared adventures their delicate romantic feelings blossom. As these bonds deepen, Althea begins to 
question the truth behind the war and the Demon Lord, and in the end she must struggle between love and the survival of the world, deciding whether her final strike will truly save everything.
